
 # 🐞 SlugBug Rules

### 🎯 Goal  
Spot real Volkswagen “Bugs” (Beetles) in the wild.  
The first player to call **8 Bugs** wins the round!  

Invite friends and family to a game of **Buggy**, or play solo to track your own sightings and achievements.

---

### 🧮 Scoring  
- Say **“Bug”** or **“Buggy”** out loud to earn **+1 point**.  
- In noisy environments, tap the **+1 button** manually.  
- Reaching **8** triggers your **reward video and celebration sound**. 🎉  

---

### 🤝 Fair Play  
- Only **real, currently visible cars** count — no photos, toys, or ads.  
- **Car lots** displaying multiple Volkswagen Beetles are **exempt** (no points).  
- **Truck carriers transporting Beetles do count** — each visible Bug is fair game!  
- One point per **unique** car (no repeats).  
- If two players call the same car, the **first clear call** wins.  

---

### 🚗 Etiquette & Safety  
- **Drivers:** keep your eyes on the road — let a passenger score for you.  
- Avoid shouting at other drivers or blocking traffic.  
- Use **Reset** to clear the round if needed.  

---

### 📸 Photos & Privacy  
- Optional photos are for fun — focus on the **car** itself.  
- Avoid capturing **faces or license plates** when possible.  

---

### 🏁 Tie-Breaker  
If the score is **7–7**, the next sighting wins the round.  

---

### 🏆 High Scorers  
Your **SlugBug achievements** are automatically tracked and displayed on the **High Scorers Page**.  
Visit: [steamintegrator.net/HighScores.html](https://steamintegrator.net/HighScores.html)

Earn achievements for cumulative Bug counts:  
- 🥉 **10 Bugs** — Rookie Spotter  
- 🥈 **50 Bugs** — Neighborhood Champion  
- 🥇 **100 Bugs** — City Cruiser  
- 🏅 **1 000 Bugs** — Legendary SlugBug Master  

Your total sightings contribute to your lifetime score — climb the leaderboard and celebrate your Buggy wins!  

---

### 🧩 Troubleshooting  
- If the mic mishears, use the **+1** button.  
- Tap the mic to re-enable speech recognition if it stops.  
- Ensure microphone and speech permissions are enabled in Settings.
